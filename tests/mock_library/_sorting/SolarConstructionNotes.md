# Solar Construction Notes

## Introduction

Solar construction involves designing and building photovoltaic (PV) systems, ranging from residential rooftops to large utility-scale solar farms.

## Key Phases of Solar Construction

Site Preparation & Civil Works Clearing, grading, and leveling the land, plus building access roads and drainage.

Foundation & Racking Installation: Driving steel I-beams or piles into the ground and bolting torque tubes and racking systems in place.

Panel Mounting: Attaching solar PV panels securely onto the mounting structures.

Electrical Work & Wiring: Connecting modules in strings, wiring them to inverters, and linking to substations and transformers.

Testing & Commissioning: Conducting independent compliance testing and quality control inspections before grid connection.
